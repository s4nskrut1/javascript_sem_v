01 — A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

A city that feels
like home.

Nagpur is one of Maharashtra's prominent cities, known for its oranges, historical monuments, educational institutions and welcoming culture.

From the peaceful dome of Deekshabhoomi to the beautiful evenings at Futala Lake, the city offers a wonderful mix of heritage, nature and urban life.
