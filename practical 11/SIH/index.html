Welcome to Smart India Hackathon

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative that encourages students to develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.

It promotes innovation, creativity and teamwork among students.

About SIH

Smart India Hackathon provides students with a platform to solve challenges presented by organizations and government departments.

Major Objectives

  • Encourage innovative thinking.
  • Develop technical and programming skills.
  • Promote teamwork and collaboration.
  • Solve real-world challenges.

Achievements and Benefits

Participating in hackathons helps students to:

  • Gain practical project experience.
  • Improve problem-solving abilities.
  • Build technical portfolios.
  • Collaborate with students from different institutions.

Learn new skills, share ideas and turn innovative concepts into practical solutions!

Career Opportunities

Hackathon experience can help students develop skills useful for internships, projects and future employment.

Important Skills:
Programming
Problem Solving
Teamwork
Innovation
